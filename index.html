Yes, it is real. Here is how you check.

  1. The box arrives sealedIt comes in the maker's retail packaging, unopened. If the box has been opened before it reaches you, tell us before you use it.
  2. The model on the box is the model on the torchThe model name printed on the box is marked on the body of the light. They should be the same.
  3. Find the serial numberLook for tiny etched digits on the body of the light, usually near the head, and for a small label carrying a number on the box. Write it down with your order number.
  4. Check the tags and what is in the boxYou should find the maker's manual and warranty card, plus every accessory listed on our product page.
  5. Not right? Send it backIf anything does not match, call us. You get a full refund. That is your right under Australian Consumer Law and we will not argue.
