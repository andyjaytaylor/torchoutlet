# What a 365nm UV torch actually shows you

A UV torch does not light things up. It makes certain materials glow. Point one around your house and you will learn things you may not have wanted to know.

## Why 365nm

Cheap UV torches run at 395nm and throw a lot of visible purple light. That purple washes out the glow you are trying to see. A 365nm light puts out far less visible light, so fluorescent marks stand out clearly.

## What people use them for

- **Banknotes and ID.** Australian banknotes have features that glow under UV, including the serial numbers. Many licences and passports have UV marks too.
- **Pet stains.** Dried urine fluoresces. Check the carpet before the bond inspection.
- **UV resin and glue.** Cures UV-setting resin, fly-tying glue and screen repair adhesive.
- **Leak detection.** UV dye in air conditioning and engine systems shows where the leak is.
- **Security marks.** Read UV pen marks on tools and gear.
- **Scorpions.** They glow under UV. Useful to know before you put your boots on.

## A warning

Do not shine a UV light into anyone's eyes, and do not stare at the LED.
