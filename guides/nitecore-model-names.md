# Nitecore model names decoded

Nitecore names its lights with letters and numbers. Once you know the letters, the range makes sense.

## The series letters

- **MH (Multitask Hybrid):** rechargeable torches with the charge port built in. [MH1C](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-mh1c), [MH12](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-mh12), [MH12GT](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-mh12gt), [MH20GT](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-mh20gt), [MH23](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-mh23).
- **MT (Multi-Task):** general purpose torches. [MT20A](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-mt20a), [MT22C](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-mt22c), [MT40GT](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-mt40gt).
- **P (Precise):** tactical torches. [P10GT](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-p10gt), [P16](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-p16).
- **HC:** headlamps. [HC30](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-hc30).
- **LA and LR:** lanterns. LR models are USB rechargeable. [LA10](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-la10), [LA30](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-la30), [LR10](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-lr10), [LR30](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-lr30).
- **T, TIP, TINI and Tube:** keychain and pocket lights. [TIP 2017](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-tip-2017), [TIP SS](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-tip-ss), [TIP CU](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-tip-cu), [TINI](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-tini), [Tube UV](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-tube-uv), [T5s](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-t5s).
- **E:** explosion-proof rated lights. EF is a torch, EH is a headlamp. [EF1](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-ef1), [EH1](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-eh1), [EH1S](https://torchoutlet.com.au/products/nitecore-eh1s).

## What GT means

GT is the long-throw version of a model. The MH12 reaches 232 metres. The MH12GT, same size, reaches 320 metres with a tighter beam.

## Are these the latest models?

Much of our range is earlier-generation Nitecore, brand new in sealed packaging. Nitecore updates its models often, so the newest versions may have a different name or charge port. The rechargeable models here charge by micro USB. That is why the prices are what they are.
