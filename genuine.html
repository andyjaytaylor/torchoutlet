Is it genuine?

Half price makes people suspicious, and it should. So do not take our word for it. Every light we sell is a genuine product, new in its sealed retail box, and you can check it yourself the day it arrives.

  1. The box arrives sealedIt comes in the maker's retail packaging, unopened. If the box has been opened before it reaches you, tell us before you use it.
  2. The model on the box is the model on the torchThe model name printed on the box is marked on the body of the light. They should be the same.
  3. Find the serial numberLook for tiny etched digits on the body of the light, usually near the head, and for a small label carrying a number on the box. Write it down with your order number.
  4. Check the tags and what is in the boxYou should find the maker's manual and warranty card, plus every accessory listed on our product page.
  5. Not right? Send it backIf anything does not match, call us. You get a full refund. That is your right under Australian Consumer Law and we will not argue.

Why is it half price?

This is stock we bought to sell, and it has been sitting in its boxes on our shelf. Most of it is the original version of a model Nitecore has since updated. Once a newer version is out, the original cannot sell at full retail, so we priced it to clear. We would rather sell it at half price than keep storing it.

Nothing has been used, opened, returned or repaired. Every light is new in the box it left the factory in.

What does retail mean on this site?

Retail is the price Australian shops sold that model for when it was on general sale. We took it from Australian retailers, using their stated recommended retail price where they gave one. Our outlet price is half of that, rounded down to the dollar. You can see both numbers for every model on the price list.

Where we could not find an Australian retail price for a model, we show our price alone and make no comparison.

Has the battery been sitting too?

Yes, and you should know that. Rechargeable models have been in storage, so give yours a full charge before first use. If it will not take a charge or hold one, that is a fault. Call us and we replace it or refund it.

Is there a manufacturer warranty?

We do not promise a manufacturer warranty on outlet stock. What you do have is Australian Consumer Law, on every sale. The light must match its description, work properly and last a reasonable time. If it does not, we repair it, replace it or refund it.
