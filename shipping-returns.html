Shipping and returns

Shipping

  • Orders ship by road from Brisbane, Queensland.
  • We deliver Australia wide.
  • Standard shipping is a flat $9.95 on any order, Australia wide. Buy one light or ten, it is still $9.95.
  • Express is $15.

Lithium batteries

Many of our lights have a lithium battery inside or in the box. We send those by road freight. Remote areas, Western Australia, the Northern Territory and Tasmania take longer.

If something is wrong

Australian Consumer Law guarantees apply to everything we sell. If a light arrives faulty, damaged or not as described, reply to your order confirmation email or message us and we will put it right.

Payment
